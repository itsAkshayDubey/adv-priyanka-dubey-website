About your lawyer

Dedicated Legal Advocate

Adv. Priyanka Dubey is a qualified legal professional with a BA.LLB and LLM degree, practicing law with unwavering commitment to justice and client welfare. With over 2 years of dedicated practice in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), she has built a reputation for providing thorough legal counsel and representation.

Her practice encompasses a wide range of legal matters in the High Court, District Court, Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal (MAT), and Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT). She approaches each case with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that her clients receive comprehensive legal support tailored to their specific needs.
